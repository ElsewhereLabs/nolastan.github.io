About

Stanford Rosenthal is a product designer and software engineer who has spent 17 years designing and building for the web, from a grassroots advocacy campaign he started as a teenager to products used by people trying to save money, find work, and make sense of their medical bills.

At 15, after Hurricane Katrina, he co-founded Levees.org with his mother to correct the public story about why New Orleans flooded. That work is the subject of the award-winning book Words Whispered in Water.

He left college to join a startup and has been an early team member at several more. As Head of Academics at Kenzie Academy, he built a UX design curriculum ranked #1 by Course Report and led a team of 45. He has worked remotely since 2016, and today builds his own products as a solo founder.
